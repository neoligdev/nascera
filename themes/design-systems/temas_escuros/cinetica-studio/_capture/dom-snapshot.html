We think beyond limits and build beyond expectations,
turning bold ideas into functional digital realities through
cutting-edge innovation and fearless experimentation.

Cinetica Studio is a creative technology studio that merges art, design, and engineering to transform bold ideas into immersive realities that move people and elevate brands.

Founded in Mexico city. Was born from the belief that creativity and technology are two expressions of the same. imagination in motion.

We operate through two main creative divisions that work hand in hand:

1.

Digital Content & CGI, focused on cinematic 3D animation, motion capture, virtual production, and digital characters that bring stories to life through design and emotion.

2.

Immersive & Interactive Experiences, dedicated to crafting large-scale installations, XR environments, and brand activations that invite audiences to participate, play, and connect inside living digital worlds.

This dual structure allows us to deliver end-to-end solutions that seamlessly blend creativity, storytelling, and advanced technology — from concept and design to programming, production, and on-site implementation. We use the most advanced tools and industry standards to ensure every project meets the highest levels of visual quality, stability, and innovation.

Cinetica has developed over 120 projects across industries such as automotive, entertainment, healthcare, tourism, and consumer goods, reaching billions of views online and engaging millions of people through immersive installations and interactive experiences around the world.

We build characters, worlds, and visuals
powered by real-time innovation.

Motion Capture & Virtual Production

We track real human movement to bring digital characters to life with cinematic realism. Perfect for films, games, and brand experiences where every gesture matters, from virtual celebrities to a lifelike performances captured inside a digital set.

CGI Characters & Digital Avatars

We design digital humans, creatures, and stylized characters that tell stories, represent brands, or interact with audiences. From virtual hosts to animated mascots, our characters create emotional connections that feel authentic and alive.

Virtual Worlds & Real-time Rendering

We build digital environments that feel tangible and alive, using real-time technology to make light, motion, and atmosphere respond naturally. Ideal for immersive brand spaces, virtual events, or cinematic storytelling that surrounds the viewer.

Fooh & Anamorphic Videos

We create optical-illusion videos that break screens and take over the physical world. Whether a product seems to leap from a billboard or a 3D object transforms a cityscape, these pieces turn heads and spark viral impact.
